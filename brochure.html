The Mechanics

How the engine works —
and why every choice was deliberate.

Five independent modules. Each documented, each validated with unit tests. No black boxes.

📈

Market Returns — Block Bootstrap

Historical monthly returns (Nifty 50, gold MCX, FD rates) are sampled in contiguous 6-month blocks, not individually. This preserves crash clustering — bad months follow bad months, as they do in reality.

Why not parametric (log-normal)?
Assumes returns are independent draws from a normal distribution. Systematically underestimates how bad a bad year actually is. 2008 and March 2020 were not one-month events.
💼

Income Model — Sector Salary Data

Income grows using real sector increment data (EY, WTW, Aon surveys). IT software sector: ~10–11% p.a. BFSI: ~9%. Manufacturing: ~8%. Four performance tiers multiply the sector rate: 0.6× to 1.6×.

Why not flat growth?
A flat 8% income growth for an IT top performer and a manufacturing average performer is wildly different in reality. Sector data makes the income trajectory honest.
🏛️

Tax Model — India New Regime, July 2024

Tax is applied within each simulation path, not as a post-hoc deduction. LTCG: 12.5% on equity and gold gains. FD interest: taxed at income slab rate. 4% health and education cess on all tax.

Why within-path, not post-hoc?
Because compounding and taxation interact. Applying tax after compounding overstates returns — sometimes significantly over 20 years.
☕

Habit Cost Model — Category Creep

Habits don't cost the same each year. Prices rise, and lifestyle habits inflate with income. Category-specific creep rates are applied annually on top of CPI: food delivery +2%, OTT +3%, travel +4%.

Why not flat CPI?
Swiggy prices have risen faster than CPI. So have Netflix plans, Uber fares, and restaurant meals. Flat CPI understates the real long-run cost of lifestyle inflation.

Sensitivity Analysis — One-at-a-time perturbation

Which lever matters most? After each run, the engine perturbs four variables by a small amount and ranks them by impact on your final P50 portfolio.

Artificial Intelligence

RupeeRipple uses open-source Llama 3.1 (8B) served through Groq's free inference API — 14,400 requests/day, low latency, no credit card required. We chose Groq over Gemini (regional quota limits) and OpenAI (no meaningful free tier) because the AI features should work out of the box, not after a billing setup.

✨ Feature 1

Plain-English Habit Parser

Type your habits naturally. The AI extracts structured data — name, rupee cost per occurrence, frequency, category — and shows a selectable checklist. You choose what to add.

"I spend ₹200 on chai every morning and order Swiggy twice a week, around ₹400 each time. I also pay ₹649 for Spotify."

→ 3 habits parsed, pre-filled, ready to simulate.

Why not a rule-based parser?

Rules cannot handle "we split Netflix with two friends, my share is roughly ₹133" or "I go to a dhaba maybe 4-5 times a week, ₹80–100 each." The LLM handles vague amounts, division, and irregular phrasing gracefully. A regex would miss them.

Model: llama-3.1-8b-instant · temp 0.1 · validated JSON output
🤖 Feature 2

AI Result Narrator

After each simulation, a 2–3 sentence personalised insight appears — written in plain English, anchored to real Indian benchmarks, specific to your numbers.

"Your weekend dining habit (₹6,500/mo) represents 68% of your opportunity cost — redirected to a balanced portfolio, it grows to ₹31L by age 48, roughly the down payment on a 2BHK in Nashik. Design decisions

Fires asynchronously — results appear immediately, insight appears 1–2 seconds later. Never blocks the UI. If the API fails, the insight card simply doesn't appear; simulation results are unaffected. Anchors to India-specific references: college fees, cars, flat downpayments, retirement corpus.

What We're Honest About

Known limitations — documented in the app.

Thin tail sample

25 years of Nifty data is a thin sample for estimating P10/P90. Tail percentiles may be unstable. Disclosed in the assumptions panel.

Tax approximation

Blended effective rate, not lot-by-lot FIFO tracking. Directionally correct; error is disclosed. Does not model 80C/ELSS deductions.

No life events

Marriage, home loan, illness, job loss — none modelled. The simulation assumes you keep investing uninterrupted. Stated clearly on every results page.

Stationary correlations

Equity-gold-FD correlations are assumed constant over the horizon. In crises they change. This is the standard limitation of all bootstrap approaches.
